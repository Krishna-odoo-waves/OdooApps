Google Review Integration

The Google Review Integration module allows you to connect multiple Google Business accounts with Odoo. Customer reviews using scheduled cron jobs. Reviews are displayed in a structured inside Odoo for easy monitoring and analysis.

  • Features
  • Working

Key Features

Google Account Integration
  • * Supports multiple Google Business accounts.
    * Securely connect and manage all accounts from Odoo.
    * Each account is handled independently.
Location Synchronization
  • * Fetches business locations based on selected Google account.
    * Keeps location data synced with Google Business Profile.
Review Fetching
  • * Fetches customer reviews based on selected locations.
    * Reviews are displayed in Odoo in a structured.
    * Supports ratings, comments, and reviewer details.
Automatic Review Replies
  • * Automatically replies to reviews based on predefined rules.
    * Reply logic can depend on rating conditions.
    * Ensures consistent and timely customer responses.
Scheduled Cron Synchronization
  • * Reviews and replies are synchronized automatically using cron jobs.
    * No manual intervention required.
    * Keeps data always up to date.
Centralized Review Management
  • * Manage reviews across multiple accounts and locations from one place.
    * Improves visibility and customer engagement efficiency.

Line Item Image for Sale|Invoice|Purchase

The Line Item Image for Sale, Invoice, and Purchase Order Lines module provides a simple but powerful enhancement to Odoo's standard order and invoice views. By displaying product images within the line items of Sales Orders, Invoices, and Purchase Orders, this module makes it easier for users to recognize products at a glance.
